The Antiparticle for macOS (universal: Apple Silicon and Intel, macOS 10.13 or later)
=====================================================================================

Open TheAntiparticle-<version>-macOS.pkg. It installs AU, VST3 and CLAP (Customize lets you leave
formats out):
  - AU   -> /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/The Antiparticle.component
  - VST3 -> /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/The Antiparticle.vst3
  - CLAP -> /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/CLAP/The Antiparticle.clap

The installer is not notarized by Apple (that needs a paid developer account), so macOS stops it
the first time:
  - macOS 15 and later: open it once, then System Settings > Privacy & Security > "Open Anyway".
  - Earlier macOS: right-click (Control-click) the .pkg, choose Open, then Open.
Restart your DAW afterwards (Logic rescans AUs at launch).

To check the download, compare its SHA-256 with SHA256SUMS.txt on the Releases page:
  shasum -a 256 TheAntiparticle-<version>-macOS.pkg
