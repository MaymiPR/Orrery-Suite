The Antiparticle for Windows (64-bit)
=====================================

Run TheAntiparticle-<version>-Windows-Setup.exe. It installs:
  - VST3 to C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\The Antiparticle.vst3
  - CLAP to C:\Program Files\Common Files\CLAP\The Antiparticle.clap
  - optionally the standalone app to C:\Program Files\The Antiparticle
Then rescan plug-ins in your DAW. Uninstall from Settings > Apps > The Antiparticle.

The installer is not code-signed, so Windows SmartScreen may warn: click "More info", then "Run anyway".
To check the download, compare its SHA-256 with SHA256SUMS.txt on the Releases page:
  certutil -hashfile TheAntiparticle-<version>-Windows-Setup.exe SHA256
