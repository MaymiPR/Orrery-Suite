Orrery Suite for Windows (64-bit)
=================================

Run OrrerySuite-<version>-Windows-Setup.exe. It installs every Orrery Suite plug-in:
  - VST3 to C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\Orrery Suite
  - CLAP to C:\Program Files\Common Files\CLAP\Orrery Suite
  - optionally the standalone apps to C:\Program Files\Orrery Suite
Pick "Choose plug-ins and formats" to leave some out, then rescan plug-ins in your DAW.
Uninstall from Settings > Apps > Orrery Suite.

The installer is not code-signed, so Windows SmartScreen may warn: click "More info", then "Run anyway".
To check the download, compare its SHA-256 with SHA256SUMS.txt on the Releases page:
  certutil -hashfile OrrerySuite-<version>-Windows-Setup.exe SHA256
