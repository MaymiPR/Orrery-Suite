Orrery Suite for Linux (64-bit x86, built on Ubuntu 22.04)
==========================================================

In this folder run:   sh install-linux.sh
It copies every Orrery Suite plug-in to ~/.vst3/Orrery Suite and ~/.clap/Orrery Suite. Then rescan
plug-ins in your DAW (Reaper, Bitwig, Ardour...). Remove them again with:  sh install-linux.sh --uninstall

Needs glibc 2.35 or later, X11 (XWayland works), libasound2 and libfreetype6.
To check the download, compare its SHA-256 with SHA256SUMS.txt on the Releases page:
  sha256sum OrrerySuite-<version>-Linux.tar.gz
